Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tax calculator updated for 2025-26?

Yes, it uses the latest FY 2025-26 slabs for both Old and New regimes, including standard deduction and 87A rebate.

Which regime is better for me?

It depends on your deductions. If you claim 80C, 80D, HRA, the Old Regime may save more. Otherwise, New Regime is usually better.

What is the standard deduction?

₹50,000 under Old Regime and ₹75,000 under New Regime for FY 2025-26.

Is my salary data stored?

No. All calculations happen in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.

Does it include cess?

Yes, 4% health and education cess is included in the final tax amount.

Can I use it for freelance income?

It's designed for salaried individuals. For business income, consult a CA.
