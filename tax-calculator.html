Which Regime Should You Choose?

  • Choose New Regime if: Your total deductions (80C + 80D + HRA + home loan) are less than roughly ₹3.75 lakh. This is common for freshers, IT professionals with few investments, and those without home loans.
  • Choose Old Regime if: You claim significant deductions — maxed-out 80C (₹1.5L), 80D (₹25K-50K), HRA exemption, home loan interest (up to ₹2L), and NPS (₹50K). Salaried employees with home loans often save more here.

What Is Marginal Relief?

If your income is just above ₹12 lakh under the New Regime, you may end up paying more tax than the extra income you earned. Marginal relief fixes this — your tax is capped at the amount by which your income exceeds ₹12 lakh. Our calculator applies this automatically for income between ₹12L and ₹12.75L.

Which regime is better for me?

It depends on your deductions. If you claim 80C, 80D, HRA, home loan interest — Old Regime may save more. Otherwise, New Regime is usually better.

What is the standard deduction?

₹50,000 under Old Regime and ₹75,000 under New Regime for FY 2025-26.

What is Section 87A rebate?

Under New Regime, if taxable income ≤ ₹12,00,000, you get full rebate up to ₹60,000. Under Old Regime, if income ≤ ₹5,00,000, rebate up to ₹12,500.

What is marginal relief?

For income slightly above ₹12L in New Regime, marginal relief ensures your tax doesn't exceed your extra income. We calculate this automatically.
