Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tax calculator updated for 2025-26?

Yes, it uses the latest FY 2025-26 slabs for both Old and New regimes, including standard deduction, 87A rebate, surcharge, and marginal relief.

Which regime is better for me?

It depends on your deductions. If you claim 80C, 80D, HRA, home loan interest — Old Regime may save more. Otherwise, New Regime is usually better.

What is the standard deduction?

₹50,000 under Old Regime and ₹75,000 under New Regime for FY 2025-26.

What is Section 87A rebate?

Under New Regime, if taxable income ≤ ₹12,00,000, you get full rebate up to ₹60,000. Under Old Regime, if income ≤ ₹5,00,000, rebate up to ₹12,500.

What is marginal relief?

For income slightly above ₹12L in New Regime, marginal relief ensures your tax doesn't exceed your extra income. We calculate this automatically.

Is my salary data stored?

No. All calculations happen in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved to any server.

Does it include cess and surcharge?

Yes, 4% health and education cess is included. Surcharge is applied for income above ₹50L.

Can I use it for freelance income?

It's designed for salaried individuals. For business income, consult a CA as rules differ.
