Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?

No. Unlike iLovePDF (25 files per merge on free) or Smallpdf (daily limits), we have no file count limit. Merge as many PDFs as your browser can handle.

Can I split a PDF by custom page ranges like 1-3, 7, 10-12?

Yes. Our Split tool accepts custom page ranges. Enter "1-3, 7, 10-12" and we extract exactly those pages into a new PDF. This is a paid feature in many tools.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens in your browser using pdf-lib. Your files never leave your device. Unlike Smallpdf and iLovePDF, which upload files to their servers and delete them after 1-2 hours.

Is there a file size limit?

No hard limit. Since processing is local, the only constraint is your browser memory. Files up to 100 MB+ typically work fine.

Will merging or splitting reduce PDF quality?

No. Our tool uses lossless copying — original pages are preserved exactly. Fonts, images, and formatting remain intact.
