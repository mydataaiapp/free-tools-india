1. Introduction

Welcome to FreeToolIndia ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit www.freetoolindia.com (the "Site").

By using our Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be crystal clear about what we don't collect:

  • ❌ We don't store your resume data — it's saved in your browser only (Resume Builder)
  • ❌ We don't store your notes — they live in your browser's localStorage (Notepad)
  • ❌ We don't store your passwords — they never leave your device (Password Checker)
  • ❌ We don't upload your images — background removal runs in your browser (Background Remover)
  • ❌ We don't require accounts, emails, or personal information
  • ❌ We don't sell your data to anyone

3. Information We May Collect

3.1 Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, we may automatically collect limited technical information:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited on our site
  • Time and date of visits
  • Referring website (how you found us)

This information is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.

3.2 Cookies

We may use minimal cookies for:

  • Remembering your preferences
  • Basic analytics (understanding which tools are popular)
  • Improving site performance

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Our tools will still work.

4. How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used only to:

  • Improve our tools and user experience
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Understand which features are most useful
  • Protect against abuse and spam

5. Third-Party Services

Some of our tools use third-party services:

5.1 Password Breach Check

Our Password Strength Checker uses the Have I Been Pwned API with k-anonymity. Only the first 5 characters of your password's SHA-1 hash are sent — your actual password never leaves your browser.

5.2 Background Remover

Our Background Remover uses an AI library loaded from a CDN (jsDelivr). The AI model runs locally in your browser. Your images are not uploaded.

5.3 PDF Text Extraction

Our ATS Scanner uses PDF.js (loaded from cdnjs) to extract text from PDF files directly in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.

5.4 Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand site usage. We do not use invasive tracking or sell data.

6. Data Security

Because most of our tools run entirely in your browser, your sensitive data (resumes, notes, passwords) never reaches our servers. This is the strongest possible protection — your data simply stays with you.

For the limited data we do collect (analytics), we use industry-standard security measures.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

Since we collect very little data, most of these rights are automatically satisfied. For any requests, contact us at privacy@freetoolindia.com.

9. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are in the EU, you have rights under GDPR. Our lawful basis for processing limited analytics data is legitimate interest (improving our service). You may object to this processing at any time.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Site means you accept the updated policy.
