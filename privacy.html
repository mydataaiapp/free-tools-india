1. Introduction

Welcome to FreeToolIndia ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit www.freetoolindia.com (the "Site").

By using our Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

Who we are: FreeToolIndia is a free online tools platform available worldwide. Our data controller contact is privacy@freetoolindia.com.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be crystal clear about what we don't collect:

  • ❌ We don't store your resume data — it's saved in your browser only (Resume Builder)
  • ❌ We don't store your notes — they live in your browser's localStorage (Notepad)
  • ❌ We don't store your passwords — they never leave your device (Password Checker)
  • ❌ We don't upload your images — background removal runs in your browser (Background Remover)
  • ❌ We don't require accounts, emails, or personal information
  • ❌ We don't sell your data to anyone

3. Information We May Collect

3.1 Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, we may automatically collect limited technical information:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited on our site
  • Time and date of visits
  • Country/region (for analytics, not precise location)
  • Referring website (how you found us)

This information is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.

3.2 Cookies

We may use minimal cookies for:

  • Remembering your preferences (e.g., dark mode)
  • Basic analytics (understanding which tools are popular)
  • Improving site performance

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Our tools will still work.

4. How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used only to:

  • Improve our tools and user experience
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Understand which features are most useful
  • Protect against abuse and spam

5. Third-Party Services

Some of our tools use third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

5.1 Password Breach Check (Have I Been Pwned)

Our Password Strength Checker uses the Have I Been Pwned API with k-anonymity. Only the first 5 characters of your password's SHA-1 hash are sent — your actual password never leaves your browser. View their privacy policy.

5.2 Background Remover (jsDelivr CDN)

Our Background Remover uses an AI library loaded from jsDelivr CDN. The AI model runs locally in your browser. Your images are not uploaded. View jsDelivr's privacy policy.

5.3 PDF Text Extraction (cdnjs)

Our ATS Scanner uses PDF.js (loaded from cdnjs) to extract text from PDF files directly in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device. View Cloudflare's privacy policy.

5.4 AI Chat Assistant

Our AI chat widget uses a secure proxy to a third-party AI provider. Messages you send to the chat may be processed by the AI provider to generate responses. Do not share sensitive personal information in the chat.

5.5 Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand site usage. We do not use invasive tracking or sell data.

6. Data Security

Because most of our tools run entirely in your browser, your sensitive data (resumes, notes, passwords) never reaches our servers. This is the strongest possible protection — your data simply stays with you.

For the limited data we do collect (analytics), we use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption.

7. Data Retention

Browser-based tool data: Your resumes, notes, and preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. We have no access to this data, and it remains until you clear your browser data.

Analytics data: Aggregated analytics data may be retained for up to 26 months for trend analysis, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Contact emails: If you email us, we retain the email for as long as needed to resolve your query, then delete it.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. This complies with COPPA (US) and GDPR-K (EU). If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Your Rights (Global)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to access — request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data
  • Right to restrict processing — limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Right to opt out of sale — we don't sell data, but you can still opt out
  • Right to lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority

Since we collect very little data, most of these rights are automatically satisfied. For any requests, contact us at privacy@freetoolindia.com.

10. GDPR Compliance (EU & UK Users)

If you are in the EU or UK, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR.

Lawful basis: Our lawful basis for processing limited analytics data is legitimate interest (improving our service). You may object to this processing at any time by contacting us or disabling cookies.

International transfers: Our servers and third-party services may process data outside the EU/UK. Where this happens, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Data Protection Officer: For GDPR-related queries, contact dpo@freetoolindia.com.

11. CCPA/CPRA Compliance (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to know what personal information we collect
  • Right to delete personal information we hold
  • Right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights, email privacy@freetoolindia.com with the subject "CCPA Request."

12. DPDP Act (India Users)

If you are in India, you have rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act):

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure of data no longer needed
  • Right to grievance redressal — contact our Grievance Officer

Grievance Officer: For DPDP-related queries, contact grievance@freetoolindia.com.

13. Changes to This Policy
