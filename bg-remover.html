Frequently Asked Questions

Is this background remover really free?

Yes, 100% free forever. No watermark, no sign-up, no limits on the number of images.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs entirely in your browser. Your image never leaves your device. We can't see your images even if we wanted to.

Can I process multiple images at once?

Yes! Upload multiple images and click "Process All" to run batch background removal. Perfect for bulk editing.

Why does the first use take longer?

The AI model (about 40MB) is downloaded once and cached by your browser. Future uses are much faster.

What image formats are supported?

PNG, JPG, and JPEG. Output can be downloaded as PNG (with transparency) or JPG (with solid background).

Can I change the background color?

Yes! After removal, choose from transparent, white, black, or a custom color of your choice.

Is there a file size limit?

No strict limit, but images under 5MB work best. Very large images may be slow due to browser memory.

Can I use the result commercially?

Yes. The output is 100% yours to use anywhere, including commercial projects.
