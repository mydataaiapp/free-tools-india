Check If Your Password Is Strong Enough — Free & Private

Weak passwords are the #1 cause of account hacks. A recent study found that over 80% of data breaches involve weak or reused passwords. If your password is in a known breach or easy to guess, attackers can access your email, bank, social media, and more within minutes.

Our free password strength checker analyzes your password in real time — giving you an entropy score, estimated crack time, pattern issues, and breach status. Unlike other tools, we never send your password to any server. Everything runs in your browser.

What Our Password Checker Analyzes

  • Entropy Score: Measured in bits — 60+ is good, 80+ is strong, 100+ is excellent
  • Length: 12+ characters is recommended for modern security
  • Time to Crack: Estimated against a modern GPU attack (100 billion guesses/second)
  • Breach Status: Checks against Have I Been Pwned database using k-anonymity
  • Pattern Detection: Sequences (123, abc), repeats (aaa), keyboard patterns (qwerty)
  • Common Password Check: Against a database of millions of leaked passwords

Who Is This For?

This free password checker is built for Indian users — whether you're securing your Gmail, Facebook, Instagram, bank account, UPI apps, or work email. Most people reuse the same password across multiple sites, which means one breach can compromise everything. Use this tool to check your passwords, then generate strong, unique ones for each account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my password safe here?

Yes, 100%. Your password never leaves your browser. It's not stored, logged, or sent to any server. We have no way to see it.

How does breach detection work?

It uses k-anonymity with the Have I Been Pwned API. Your password is hashed with SHA-1, and only the first 5 characters of the hash are sent. The server returns a list of matching hashes — your actual password is never transmitted.

What is a good entropy score?

60+ bits is good, 80+ bits is strong, 100+ bits is very strong. Aim for 12+ characters with mixed case, numbers, and symbols.

What is "time to crack"?

It estimates how long a modern GPU-based attack would take to brute-force your password. Based on 100 billion guesses per second.

Should I change a breached password?

Yes, immediately. If your password appears in a breach, change it on all sites where you use it and enable 2FA.

Are generated passwords safe?

Yes. They're generated using your browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues). Nothing is sent to any server.

What is a passphrase?

A passphrase is a series of random words (like "correct-horse-battery-staple"). They're easier to remember and very secure.

Do you store or log passwords?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Even our breach check uses k-anonymity so your password stays private.
