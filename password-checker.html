Frequently Asked Questions

Is my password safe here?

Yes. Your password never leaves your browser. It is not stored, logged, or sent to any server.

How does breach detection work?

It uses k-anonymity with the Have I Been Pwned API. Only the first 5 characters of your password's SHA-1 hash are sent — never the password itself.

What is a good entropy score?

Above 70 bits is strong, above 90 bits is very strong. Aim for 12+ characters with mixed case, numbers, and symbols.

What does "time to crack" mean?

It estimates how long a modern computer would take to brute-force your password.

Should I change a breached password?

Yes, immediately. If your password appears in a breach, change it on all sites where you use it.

Do you store or log passwords?

No. Everything runs in your browser. We have no way to see your password.
