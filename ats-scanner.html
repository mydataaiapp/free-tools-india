Why Your Resume Needs an ATS Check

Over 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS (Applicant Tracking System) before a human recruiter ever sees them. Companies worldwide — from Fortune 500s to startups — use ATS software to filter thousands of applications. If your resume isn't optimized for ATS, it doesn't matter how qualified you are — you'll never get the interview call.

Our free ATS resume scanner analyzes your resume the same way these systems do. It checks for standard section headings, keyword match with the job description, action verbs, quantified achievements, formatting issues, and more — giving you a score out of 100 with clear, actionable fixes.

Multi-Platform ATS Simulation

Different companies use different ATS platforms, and they don't all read resumes the same way. Our scanner simulates three major parsing approaches:

  • Workday (used by 40%+ of Fortune 500): Strict parser, skips headers/footers, penalizes creative formats, weights job titles heavily.
  • Taleo (Oracle): Strictest literal keyword matching. Auto-reject via Req Rank if exact keywords are missing.
  • iCIMS: ML-based semantic matching. More forgiving, understands context and synonyms.

This gives you a realistic range of scores — not a single misleading number.

What Our ATS Scanner Checks

  • Length: Ideal resume length (300-800 words) for ATS parsing
  • Standard Sections: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Contact
  • Contact Info: Email, phone, LinkedIn profile
  • Formatting: Tabs, special characters, HTML tags, long lines
  • Action Verbs: Led, developed, improved, launched, achieved...
  • Quantified Results: Percentages, amounts, team sizes, timeframes
  • Keyword Match: Against your target job description (fuzzy/stemmed matching)
  • Readability: Average word length and clarity
