Why Your Resume Needs an ATS Check

Over 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS (Applicant Tracking System) before a human recruiter ever sees them. Companies in India — from TCS, Infosys, and Wipro to startups in Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Mumbai — use ATS software to filter thousands of applications. If your resume isn't optimized for ATS, it doesn't matter how qualified you are — you'll never get the interview call.

Our free ATS resume scanner analyzes your resume the same way these systems do. It checks for standard section headings, keyword match with the job description, action verbs, quantified achievements, formatting issues, and more — giving you a score out of 100 with clear, actionable fixes.

What Our ATS Scanner Checks

  • Length: Ideal resume length (300-700 words) for ATS parsing
  • Standard Sections: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Contact
  • Contact Info: Email, phone, LinkedIn profile
  • Formatting: Tabs, special characters, HTML tags, long lines
  • Action Verbs: Led, developed, improved, launched, achieved...
  • Quantified Results: Percentages, amounts, team sizes, timeframes
  • Keyword Match: Against your target job description
  • Readability: Average word length and clarity
