What is an ATS?

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software companies use to scan and filter resumes before a human sees them. About 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS.

How is my score calculated?

Based on 8 factors: length, sections, contact info, formatting, action verbs, quantified results, keyword match, and readability.

Is my resume uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Your resume text never leaves your device.

What is a good ATS score?

80+ is excellent, 60-79 is good, below 60 needs improvement. Aim for 80+ for best results.

Can I use this for any job?

Does it check for specific keywords?

Yes! When you paste a job description, we extract important keywords and show you which ones are missing from your resume.
