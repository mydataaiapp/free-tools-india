How Can We Help?

Whether you have a question about one of our tools, want to suggest a new feature, or need to report an issue — we're here to help. FreeToolIndia is built and maintained by a small team that genuinely cares about Indian users. Your feedback directly shapes what we build next.

What to Include in Your Email

To help us respond faster, please include:

  • For bug reports: The tool name, what you did, what happened, and what you expected.
  • For suggestions: The tool idea and how it would help you.
  • For questions: The specific tool and your question.
  • Your browser and device (optional but helpful).

⚡ We typically respond within 48 hours on business days.
