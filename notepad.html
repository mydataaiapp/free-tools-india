Frequently Asked Questions

Is this notepad really free?

Yes, 100% free forever. No sign-up, no ads, no premium plan, unlimited notes.

What menus does this notepad have?

Just like Windows Notepad, we have File, Edit, Insert, View, and Help menus with all the essential options like Save, Find & Replace, Undo/Redo, Date/Time insert, Dark Mode, Font size, and more.

Where are my notes saved?

In your browser's localStorage. They never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server.

Can I create multiple notes?

Yes! Click "+ New Note" or File → New Note to create unlimited notes. All notes appear in the left sidebar.

Does it have Find & Replace?

Yes! Press Ctrl+H or go to Edit → Find & Replace. You can search within the current note and replace text easily.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the page loads, you can keep typing even without internet. Everything saves locally.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes! Use View → Toggle Dark Mode or click the top-right button. Your theme preference is saved.

Can I access my notes on another device?

No, notes are saved per-browser. Use File → Export All Notes to download them as .txt and transfer manually.

What keyboard shortcuts are supported?

Ctrl+S (Save), Ctrl+F (Find), Ctrl+H (Replace), Ctrl+N (New), Ctrl+D (Download), F5 (Date/Time), Ctrl+Z (Undo), Ctrl+Y (Redo), and more. See Help → Keyboard Shortcuts for full list.
