Frequently Asked Questions

Is this notepad really free?

Yes, 100% free forever. No sign-up, no ads, no premium plan, no limits on the number of notes.

Where are my notes saved?

Directly in your browser using localStorage. They stay on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

Can I create multiple notes?

Yes! Click "+ New Note" to create unlimited notes. All notes are listed in the left sidebar for quick access.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the page loads, you can keep typing and creating notes even without internet. Everything saves locally.

Can I access my notes on another device?

No, notes are saved per-browser. Use the Export button to download them as .txt files and transfer manually.

Is there a character limit?

No strict limit, but very large notes (over 5MB total) may slow down your browser. For typical notes, there's no issue.

Can I search within my notes?

Yes. Use the search box in the sidebar to filter notes by title or content.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes! Click the "🌙 Dark Mode" button in the top bar to switch between light and dark themes.

Can I recover deleted notes?

No, once deleted they're gone. Always export important notes as backup before deleting.
