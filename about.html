About FreeToolIndia

Privacy-first free tools built for India 🇮🇳

Who We Are

FreeToolIndia is a collection of simple, fast, and 100% free online tools designed specifically for Indian users. We believe that useful tools shouldn't cost money, require sign-ups, or compromise your privacy.

Every tool on this website runs directly in your browser. That means your files, notes, resumes, and passwords never leave your device. We don't have servers storing your data, we don't track you, and we don't sell anything.

🎯 Our mission: Give every Indian access to professional-grade tools — completely free, forever.
