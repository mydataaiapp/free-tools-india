Frequently Asked Questions

Is this resume builder really free?

Yes, 100% free forever. No hidden charges, no premium plan, no credit card required. Download unlimited resumes.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can build and download your resume without any sign-up, login, or email verification.

Is my personal data safe?

Absolutely. All data is saved only in your browser using localStorage. Nothing is uploaded to any server. We can't see your resume even if we wanted to.

Will my resume pass ATS screening?

Yes, this builder uses ATS-friendly formatting — simple headings, no tables, no images in text areas, standard fonts. Use our ATS Scanner to verify your score.

How is the ATS score calculated?

The built-in ATS score checks completeness, contact info, action verbs, quantified achievements, and section presence — giving you real-time feedback as you type.

Can I edit my resume later?

Yes. Your data is auto-saved in your browser. Just open this page again in the same browser and continue editing.

What format is the downloaded resume?

You can download it as PDF using the built-in print option (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P). The layout is clean, single-column, and ATS-compatible.

Can I use this resume for any job?

Yes. It works for any industry — software, marketing, finance, healthcare, education, and more. Just adjust your skills and experience.
