Free ATS Resume Builder — Create a Professional Resume in Minutes

Did you know that over 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS (Applicant Tracking System) before a human recruiter ever sees them? In India, top companies like TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Amazon, Flipkart, and thousands of startups use ATS software to filter job applications. If your resume isn't ATS-friendly, it doesn't matter how qualified you are — you won't get the interview call.

Our free ATS Resume Builder solves this problem. It guides you through building a clean, single-column, ATS-optimized resume — with a live preview, built-in ATS score, and instant PDF download. No sign-up, no watermark, and your data never leaves your browser.

What Makes Our Resume Builder ATS-Friendly?

  • Single-column layout: ATS systems can't read multi-column resumes properly
  • Standard fonts: Times New Roman, single-column, clean headings
  • Standard section names: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills — exactly what ATS looks for
  • No tables, no images, no graphics: All text-based, easy for ATS to parse
  • Built-in ATS score: Real-time feedback on completeness, action verbs, quantified results

Pro Tips for a Better ATS Score

  • Use action verbs: Led, Developed, Increased, Reduced, Launched
  • Add numbers: "Increased sales by 25%", "Led team of 5", "Saved ₹10 lakh annually"
  • Keep it 1-2 pages (300-700 words)
  • Include keywords from the job description naturally
  • Use a professional email like firstname.lastname@gmail.com
  • Add your LinkedIn profile URL
  • Use our ATS Scanner to double-check your resume before applying
